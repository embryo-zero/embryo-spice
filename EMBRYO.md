# Embryo Spice

Embryo Spice is Embryo-Zero's fork of [ngspice](https://ngspice.sourceforge.io) (8bf4803, commit 8bf4803). It simulates analog circuits.
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
